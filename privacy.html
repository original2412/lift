Lift — Privacy policy

Lift is a personal workout tracker. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

What the app stores

Your routines, workouts, exercises, measurements and settings are stored on your phone. They are only sent anywhere if you turn on syncing or backup.

Sign in with Google

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Lift receives your Google account ID, name and email address. They are used only to identify you so your workout data can be synced to your account and restored on another phone. Lift does not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else in your Google account.

Where synced data is kept

Synced data is stored on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers / Durable Objects), keyed to your account, and used only to provide syncing. With the recovery-code backup option instead, data is encrypted on your phone before upload and cannot be read on the server.

Rest-timer notifications

If you allow notifications, a push subscription for your device and the time your rest ends are sent to the server so it can notify you. They are deleted when the notification is sent or cancelled.

What we don’t do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Signing out stops syncing. To delete your synced data, email the address below and it will be removed.
